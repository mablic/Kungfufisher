2. Purpose and target audience
Kung Fu Fisher is a single-player arcade fishing game inspired by Chinese wuxia, for casual players who enjoy short aiming, timing, and score challenges. It provides entertainment through catching fish, building combos, collecting martial-arts techniques, and earning stars and seals across 42 levels in seven themed regions. It is an entertainment app rather than a professional service.

3. Setup and access to main features
No account, credentials, sample files, or extra hardware are required.
- Launch and tap Begin fishing (or Continue), then Cast off.
- Hold underwater to charge, drag to aim, and release to cast. Catch fish to reach the score target. Earn one star to unlock the next level.
- Strike a floating martial-arts manual to equip a technique for three casts. The equipment badge displays remaining uses.
- Tap ? to pause and open the Fish, Skills, Rivers, and About guide. Pause/resume is also available through the pause control.
- At the end of an eligible run, an optional rewarded ad can grant 15 additional seconds, once per run. The button enables when an ad is ready. Unavailable or skipped ads do not block free retries or ordinary progression.
Core gameplay works offline; ads require internet access. Progress and settings are saved locally. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Reviewers and customers use the same release progression.

4. External services, tools, and platforms
Google AdMob provides optional rewarded ads; Google User Messaging Platform handles applicable consent/privacy forms.
