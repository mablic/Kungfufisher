# Kung Fu Fisher Support

Need help with a cast, a level, or an unexpected problem? Contact **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**.

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, the level you were playing, and what happened. A screenshot or short recording can help. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal details.

## How to play

Hold anywhere underwater to charge, drag to aim, and release to cast. Longer holds reach deeper. Lead moving fish rather than aiming at where they were.

Strike a floating martial arts manual to equip a technique. Each manual grants three casts. A new manual replaces the current technique and restores three uses.

Tap the question mark during a run to pause and open the Fish, Skills, and Rivers guide. One star clears a level and unlocks the next.

## Sound and vibration

Use the Sound, Music, and Haptics controls on the home screen to change your preferences. Check your device volume and silent-mode settings if you cannot hear audio.

## Progress

Scores, level progress, and preferences are stored on your device. The current version does not provide an account-based cloud-save service. Deleting the app may remove local progress.

## Problems running the app

Close and reopen the app, and check for an available update. Contact support before deleting the app if you want to preserve your local progress.
