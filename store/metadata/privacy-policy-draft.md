## Information handled by the game

The current version does not require an account. It saves game progress, scores, and sound, music, and haptic preferences locally on your device. We do not receive these local game records through an account or cloud-save service operated by us.

The game integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID and other SDK identifiers), IP addresses, device information, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. On iOS, the app does not request ATT authorization; native SDK configuration disables publisher first-party ID and ad personalization before initialization. The app requests non-personalized ads; this does not mean that ads collect no data. See [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google’s advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

Where required and configured, Google’s User Messaging Platform presents consent and privacy choices before advertising requests. When applicable, you can reopen these from “Ad privacy choices” on the game’s home screen. Gameplay does not require advertising consent. You may decline the rewarded-ad offer and retry a level for free. Completing the rewarded action grants 15 additional seconds, once per run.

The game does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone for gameplay.
